## Warranty Coverage

- The warranty cover defects in software defects, defective components, or VinFast manufacturing defects, provided the vehicle is properly used and maintained. Items listed under Warranty Exclusions are not covered.
- Replacement parts provided under warranty are the smallest individual genuine parts or components supplied by VinFast.
- Warranty coverage is valid throughout Vietnam and is provided exclusively at VinFast Authorized Service Centers and Dealers.
- Warranty repairs are performed free of charge in accordance with the applicable warranty terms and conditions.
